## Start with the selling belief

The method's exact 16 words are: "The secret to converting copy is to define the one belief, then answer these ten questions."

Define this before drafting:

> This new opportunity is the key to their desire and it's only attainable through my new mechanism.

Separate the three parts:

- **New opportunity:** what is different about the proposed route to the result.
- **Desire:** the outcome the reader already wants, including its personal significance.
- **New mechanism:** how the offer enables that route and why this provider is relevant.

Use One Belief as the test for retaining material. An interesting anecdote that does not advance the argument belongs elsewhere. Treat "only attainable" as the author's strategic formulation, not permission to assert unsupported exclusivity. If the evidence supports a narrower advantage, express that advantage accurately.

## Core decisions

### Novelty and benefit belong together

Evaldo aims to answer Q1 and Q2 on the first page. Explain why this is different and why the reader should care before a long biography or product tour. His patent-application opening makes a claimed distinction tangible. A patent application is neither an issued patent nor evidence that an indicator works.

Specificity needs support. A precise amount, time frame, or process can make a promise intelligible; precision itself does not establish truth. Do not carry historical financial or health claims into new copy as verified evidence.

### Proof remains part of the story

Use **ABT: And, But, Therefore**. Establish the relevant situation, introduce the obstacle or contradiction, and show the evidenced result and its implication. Do not merely attach a testimonial wall. A case proves only what happened in that case, under those conditions.

Use Q3 for early credibility and Q8 for the causal explanation. Neither a famous person's endorsement nor a plausible analogy replaces evidence for the mechanism.

### The real problem connects the past to the new route

Reverse-engineer Q1 to generate a Q4 hypothesis: if the difference is entering before news becomes public, the proposed obstacle is entering after the move. Then check that explanation against facts and the reader's experience.

The lecture names the **magic conversion formula**: **real problem + new mechanism = hope**. The new mechanism must actually address the obstacle. Do not manufacture a diagnosis to make the offer fit.

### Shared opposition and urgency need a basis

Q5 should grow from the audience's existing experience. A process, constraint, incentive, or conventional practice may be the shared obstacle. The book's political enemies describe its historical audience; they are not defaults for another market.

Q6 addresses inertia. Distinguish a verified deadline, an external event, and a forecast. The sources' Amazon/Bitcoin deadline changes across versions and rests on a prediction. Preserve it as an analytical example, not a template for turning speculation into certainty.

### Trust is a relevant story

Choose the strongest story supported by the speaker's actual history:

1. **I've been in your shoes:** shared struggle, discovery, change, and a credible route the reader can follow.
2. **Robin Hood:** legitimate insider experience becomes accessible to an underserved audience.
3. **Expert:** relevant work and achievements demonstrate competence.

These can combine. A chronological resume is rarely the best credibility section. Use the client's voice rather than copying the speaker's financial-promotion persona.

### Explain the mechanism before the offer carries the sale

Identify what happens, why it can affect the result, the evidence, and the conditions. Connect to familiar beliefs without treating familiarity as proof. Keep the explanation readable with ABT, a demonstration, or a compact sequence. Name the mechanism only after understanding the underlying process.

### Increase useful value, then clarify the decision

Evaldo prefers increasing value to default discounting. Build complementary bonuses that remove obstacles to using the main offer. His newsletter offer sequence is:

**Special report → subscription service → testimonials → false close/high anchor → bonuses → value stack → actual price → guarantee.**

The report-first sequence is a newsletter pattern, not a requirement for every business. Explain what is paid and what is included. Use defensible anchors and check arithmetic. State the guarantee's actual scope; refunding a subscription does not remove investment risk.

Close with **push-pull**: give genuine room to decline, then make the stakes and available assistance clear. One source pattern is **do nothing / do it yourself / let me help**. Keep alternatives fair. Do not invent disqualifications or hide meaningful choices to simulate autonomy.
